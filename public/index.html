Exhibition Guidelines

Bespoke Size Sensation

Acquiring your custom artwork requires precise measurements. Confirm your fit in 4 simple steps to ensure a flawless presentation.

01

Canvas Prep

Gently prep your natural nail surface. Lightly buff to remove natural oils and wipe clean using alcohol pads for maximum adhesive bond.

02

Scale Width

Apply soft tape across your nail bed. Mark the lateral edges with a fine tip pen, remove, and measure the span in millimeters (mm).

03

Acquisition Size

Repeat for all ten nails. Cross-reference with our NFS categories or supply your custom millimeter dimensions during collection acquisition.

04

Presentation

Apply gel pads or boutique nail adhesive. Press the master plate at a 45° angle from the cuticle line, holding for 20 seconds.

Exhibition Inquiries

Discover the technical curation behind our hand-crafted wear. Read our official curator answers to common collector inquiries.

Are Nezlin Art pieces healthy for my natural nails?
Yes, we prioritize natural plate integrity. By completely avoiding abrasive drilling, harsh chemicals, and acrylic damages typical in traditional nail salons, your natural nail remains completely healthy, breathing, and protected under the artwork.
How can home-applied sets be as durable as salon extensions?
The secret lies in the professional curation of materials and our complimentary Prep Kit. Using our high-strength non-toxic adhesive combined with the structured builder bases of Nezlin tips, the wearable art merges flawlessly with your natural nail plate, yielding 3+ weeks of extreme durability in just 10 minutes.
How long do the masterpieces last, and are they reusable?
Every set is built to be reusable. Temporary wear with sticky gel pads lasts 2-3 days (perfect for events), while standard wear with our premium glue lasts up to 21+ days. The sets can be safely removed, stored, and reused up to 5+ times, offering unmatched value compared to traditional salon treatments.
Can I request a bespoke custom design?
Absolutely. We accept custom commissions to bring your unique design visions to life. Engage the artist via our Custom Design plaque section above to define a bespoke canvas profile tailored exclusively for you.
